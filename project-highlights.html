AUROVIA Engineering Achievements and Simulation Results

An overview of the key capabilities developed in AUROVIA, bringing together Ackermann vehicle control, autonomous navigation, simulated sensors and smart urban infrastructure using ROS 2 and Gazebo.

PROJECT CONTRIBUTIONS

Key Project Achievements

Urban Environment

Eight interconnected zones and diverse road infrastructure.

Vehicle Control

Manual and autonomous Ackermann vehicle operation.

Autonomous Navigation

Destination-based movement using odometry feedback.

LiDAR and Obstacle Avoidance

Directional obstacle assessment and avoidance functionality.

Camera and Sensor Integration

Live camera imagery, IMU and GNSS interfaces.

Smart Infrastructure

Traffic signals, day/night lighting and automatic headlamps.

DEVELOPMENT OUTCOMES

Simulation Results

◆

Vehicle operation: Manual driving, steering and braking demonstrated.

◆

Autonomous navigation: Destination-based autonomous movement using odometry feedback.

◆

Camera: Real-time camera images available as the vehicle moves through the simulated environment.

◆

LiDAR: Distance processing, directional monitoring and dashboard proximity display.

◆

Obstacle avoidance: Obstacle assessment and avoidance functionality implemented in the navigation system.

◆

Traffic lights: Traffic-signal states, navigation interfaces and vehicle-response logic integrated.

◆

Dashboard: Vehicle state, camera imagery and selected sensor information presented through a unified interface.

◆

Environmental systems: Day/night transitions, street lighting and automatic headlamp operation.
